# Notice

`grill-me-sales` is adapted from **grill-me** and **grilling** by Matt Pocock, part of his skills collection at https://github.com/mattpocock/skills (checked November 24, 2026, commit d653911).

The interview method comes from his work: working a tree of open decisions in rounds, asking only the questions whose prerequisites are settled (the frontier), giving a recommended answer the user can accept with "yes," looking up facts instead of asking for them, and not acting until the user confirms shared understanding.

MKTNG added the website-first opening, the sales questions, the voice guidance and the context-document output.
